Privacy policy

This policy covers the PDF Docket Android app (me.d3sox.pdfwallet), published by D3SOX.

No data collection

PDF Docket doesn't collect, transmit or share any data. The app has no internet permission, so it can't send anything over the network. It contains no ads, analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the PDFs you import, the names and tags you assign and your settings in its private storage on your device. Other apps can't read this storage. A PDF leaves the app only when you share it with another app yourself.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything it stored. Android's cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Permissions

The app uses biometric authentication only if you turn on "Require authentication" in the settings. Android checks your fingerprint, face or PIN. The app never receives biometric data.

Ask questions or report problems in the issue tracker.
