PDF Docket

An Android app that stores your PDF tickets, passes and certificates and shows their barcodes in fullscreen. It works offline and is free and open source.

Google Play closed test

Help test PDF Docket before its Google Play release.

  1. Join the tester group
  2. Opt in and install on Google Play

Use the same Google account for both steps. Please stay opted in for at least 14 days and send feedback via GitHub Issues.

Google Play, F-Droid and GitHub builds share one signing key, so you can switch between them without reinstalling the app.
